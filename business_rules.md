# Crawdad Junction Catering: Business Rules

Author: Britton Leggett. **Fictional company, for workshop practice only.**

## Prices
- **Boiled crawfish:** $7.00 per pound (in season, roughly January through June).
- **Shrimp boil** (off-season substitute): $9.50 per pound.
- **Pounds per guest:** 3 lb per adult, 1.5 lb per child (under 12).
- **Minimum order:** 30 lb.
- **Sides package** (corn, potatoes, sausage): $4.00 per guest, optional.

## Discounts
- **Large events:** 10% off the boil (crawfish or shrimp) for 100 or more total guests. Children count as guests. The discount does **not** apply to sides.
- No other discounts exist. Do not offer group, church, military, or student discounts unless a manager approves one.

## Tax, delivery, deposit
- **Sales tax:** 9.75% on food (boil plus sides). No tax on delivery.
- **Delivery:** free within 10 miles of the shop. $2.00 per mile beyond 10 miles. Orders more than 40 miles away are declined.
- **Deposit:** 25% of the total, due at booking.
- **Rounding:** round money to the cent, half up.

## Policies
- **Booking:** at least 72 hours ahead. Same-day or next-day requests go to a manager.
- **Cancellations:** full deposit refund if cancelled 7+ days before the event; 50% refund if 3-6 days before; no refund under 3 days. Weather cancellations (named storm or tornado warning) are always fully refunded.
- **Allergies:** shellfish is in everything we boil. We cannot guarantee any item is allergen-free. Never tell a customer a dish is "safe" for an allergy.
- **Complaints:** apologize, collect the event date and details, and send to a manager. Do not promise refunds; only managers approve them.
- **Payment:** never accept card numbers by email. Direct customers to the secure payment link a manager sends.
